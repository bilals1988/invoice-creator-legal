# Privacy Policy — Invoice Creator

**Effective date:** November 9, 2026

**Developer:** Bilal B. S.  
**Contact:** bilal1988@gmail.com

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Invoice Creator ("the App") handles information when you use the Android application.

Invoice Creator is an offline invoice and quotation management application developed and published by Bilal B. S.

## 2. Information You Enter

To provide its functionality, the App allows you to enter and store information such as:

- Business name, address, logo, contact details, and tax registration information.
- Customer names, addresses, and contact details.
- Invoice and quotation details, descriptions, quantities, prices, discounts, taxes, and payment status.
- Document settings, numbering preferences, notes, and payment instructions.

This information is processed locally to create, manage, display, and export business documents.

## 3. Data Storage and Collection

The current version of Invoice Creator stores business and document information locally on your device.

The App does not require you to register an account and does not transmit your invoice, customer, or business records to developer-operated servers.

The current version does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, or developer-operated tracking services.

Google Play and your Android device may separately process installation, security, diagnostic, or platform-related information under their own policies.

## 4. Sharing and Exporting Information

Invoice Creator allows you to generate, preview, print, and share PDF documents.

When you choose to share or print a document, the information contained in that document may be transferred to the application, printer service, or recipient you select.

The App also allows you to export backup files through your device's file-management and sharing features.

These transfers are initiated by you. Information saved to another application, cloud storage service, recipient, or device may be handled according to that recipient's policies.

## 5. Backup and Restore

Invoice Creator supports manual backup and restore.

Backup files may contain your complete business records, customer information, documents, settings, and embedded business logos.

**Backup files are not encrypted by the App.** You should keep them in a secure location and avoid sharing them with unauthorized parties.

The App does not automatically upload your backup files to a developer-operated cloud service.

Restoring a backup replaces the App's existing saved records after confirmation.

## 6. Data Security

The App uses Android's application-private storage to store its local database.

We take reasonable measures within the App to limit unintended access, but no device or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

If you export or share a document or backup, copies stored outside the App are no longer controlled by the App.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored in Invoice Creator remains on your device until you delete it, replace it through a backup restoration, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App.

You can delete supported individual records using the App's available controls. Removing the App or clearing its storage generally removes locally stored application data, subject to the behavior of your device and any independently saved or transferred copies.

Deleting local data does not automatically remove PDF documents or backup files that you previously exported or shared.

Because the App does not maintain developer-operated user accounts or a cloud database, the developer does not provide a remote account-deletion service for locally stored records.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Invoice Creator is intended for business and professional document management and is not designed specifically for children.

## 9. Third-Party Services

The App uses Android system features and software libraries to provide functionality such as local storage, file selection, PDF generation, printing, and sharing.

When you choose an external application or service to open, store, print, or share a document, that external provider may process the information you provide according to its own privacy practices.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data-handling practices change.

Updated versions will be published at the official Privacy Policy URL with a revised effective date.

If future versions introduce advertising, analytics, online accounts, cloud synchronization, or payment-related services, the policy will be updated to reflect those changes before or when they become available.
